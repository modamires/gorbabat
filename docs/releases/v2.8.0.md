# Gorbabat v2.8.0

## Automatic return to real price

The 10-tap easter egg now has a full recovery cycle:

1. Every 10 taps reduces the displayed Toman price by 10,000.
2. The reduced price stays visible for 10 seconds.
3. After 10 seconds, the number animates upward back to the real source/API price.
4. The upward count animation lasts about 1.2 seconds.

If multiple 10-tap milestones are queued (20, 30, ... taps), all pending
10,000-Toman drops finish first. The 10-second recovery timer begins after the
final queued drop.

If another 10-tap milestone happens while the price is waiting to recover,
the old recovery timer is cancelled and a fresh 10-second timer starts after
the new reduction completes.

If a milestone is reached while the upward recovery animation is already
running, that new reduction is queued and starts immediately after recovery.

The backend/raw market price is never modified.
